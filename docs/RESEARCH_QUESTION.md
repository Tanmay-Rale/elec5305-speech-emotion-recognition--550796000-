# Research Questions

## Primary
Which acoustic and temporal properties of emotional speech generalise across previously unseen speakers, and how does emotional intensity affect that generalisation?

## Secondary
Does temporal modelling provide useful speaker-independent emotion information after speaker-specific variation is controlled through grouped training and feature selection?

## Feature-selection question
Can actor-grouped nested sequential feature selection identify a compact acoustic representation that matches or improves unseen-speaker SER performance compared with the full candidate space?

## Temporal question
How much classification performance is lost when the temporal ordering of the same short-time acoustic features is destroyed?

## Interpretation boundary
Results describe recognition of acted **expressed emotion** in RAVDESS and must not be generalized to perfect inference of private internal emotion.
